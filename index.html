So what's dropshipping?

It's a way of selling where we don't keep shelves of stock ourselves. A supplier stores the products and sends them out when someone orders. Here's how that goes.

  1. You place an order

    You pick something you like and order it from us.

  2. Our supplier packs it

    We pass your order along and the supplier gets it ready.

  3. It comes to you

    The supplier ships it straight to your door.

Good to know

  • Can I buy something yet?

    Not yet. We're still setting things up.

  • Who helps if something goes wrong?

    We do. Your order is with Rox Studios, so it's on us to help sort it out.

  • Why might a parcel come from somewhere else?

    Suppliers ship orders directly, so it may not come from us. That's normal with dropshipping.
